How Driver Registration Works:

To ensure maximum passenger safety, drivers cannot register independently without document verification.

  • All driver accounts are created and verified by System Administrators.
  • Identity proof, RC book, driving license, and police clearance are audited.
  • Once approved, official login credentials and a unique QR Safety Code are issued.
